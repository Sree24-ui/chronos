connecting to engine…

A stock exchange,
running live in front of you.

CHRONOS is a matching engine: the software at the heart of every exchange that pairs people who want to buy with people who want to sell, fairly and in microseconds. Here it runs on made-up traders, so you can watch it, poke it, and measure it.

Enter the trading floorHow it works ↓

Why it matters: on a real exchange every millisecond of matching delay costs money. This project explores how the data structures inside change that.

01 — ORDERS ARRIVE

Buyers and sellers shout prices.

Each order says three things: buy or sell, how many, and the price they will accept. Thousands arrive every second.

–orders received so far

02 — PRICE FIRST, THEN TIME

The best price always goes first.

A buyer offering more beats a buyer offering less. At the same price, whoever arrived earlier is served first. The engine never breaks this rule, not even while it reorganises its own memory.

–orders waiting in the book

03 — TRADES PRINT

When prices meet, a trade happens.

The moment a buyer’s price reaches a seller’s, they trade, in part or in full. Cancels, changes and “buy at any price” orders are handled too.

–trades executed
